## Important Terminology 

In order to get a broader understanding of how Weltheas works, it is important to start off with a couple of terms. These terms must be explained to have a mutual understanding of the fundamentals of Weltheas. Keep in mind, the definition of the following terms could possibly derive from their out-of-Arcamere counterpart.

## Economy 

The Welthean Economy is centrally planned, this means that all broader decisions that affect the Welthean economy must be made by the Council.
