## Important Terminology 

In order to get a broader understanding of how Weltheas works, it is important to start off with a couple of terms. These terms must be explained to have a mutual understanding of the fundamentals of Weltheas. Keep in mind, the definition of the following terms could possibly derive from their out-of-Arcamere counterpart.

- Welthean Economy (abbr. WE) = The total economy of the Free City of Weltheas
- Welthean Central Bank (abbr. WCB) = the sole economic institute that deals with the Welthean Economy
- Welthean Characteristics (abbr. WC) = permits the existence of privately operated businesses within the centrally planned economy regulated by the WCB

## Economy 

### The WCB, the Currency and the Council

The Welthean Economy (abbr. WE) operates under a centrally planned system in which gold, in both raw and processed forms, serves as the primary currency.

All major economic decisions are centralized and must be approved by the Council. Ratification requires:
- a three-fifths (3/5) majority of the full Council OR
- a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the Crisis Council, a temporary body existing exclusively during periods of declared crisis

Economic administration, regulation, and implementation are handled by the Welthean Central Bank (abbr. WCB), which functions as the sole economic institution of the state. The Central Bank operates under the authority and governance of the Council and is responsible for all matters related to currency, planning, and economic execution.

### Socialism with Welthean Characteristics

Weltheas is founded on socialist principles, under which the state guarantees access to basic necessities for all citizens. Housing, healthcare, food security, and essential public services are provided to ensure that every Welthean is protected from market instability. The government’s role in these sectors exists solely to provide for the population, not to compete with private enterprise.

Alongside this foundation exists a framework known as “Welthean Characteristics.” Under this system, private businesses are fully permitted and encouraged to exist within the broader planned economy.

Under this system, all businesses operating legally within Weltheas are required to register with the state and obtain a business permit through the Welthean Central Bank (WCB). This registration system exists to maintain transparency and allow the government to keep accurate records of economic activity. Operating a business without registration is *technically* possible, but such activity is considered part of the *black market* and is therefore illegal and punishable by death. (see section on political ideals)

Private individuals or collectives may establish and operate businesses across most sectors, provided they comply with labor laws, safety regulations, and economic guidelines. The permit system exists to ensure accountability and coordination within the Welthean economy.

In key sectors such as food and healthcare, the government maintains a strong presence to guarantee universal access. However, this presence is not exclusive. Citizens are free to open restaurants, cafés, bars, specialty food stalls, private clinics, and other enterprises alongside state providers. These private businesses should coexist with public services rather than compete against them.

The WCB remains authority to:
- issue, renew, suspend, or revoke business permits in accordance with state law
- regulate prices and wages in specific sectors where intervention is deemed necessary for public welfare
- audit registered businesses for legal, financial, and labor compliance
- impose corrective oversight or temporary intervention during periods of severe noncompliance or economic crisis

This arrangement establishes a deliberate system of “checks and balances”. Businesses rely on the WCB for legal existence, access to the Welthean currency, … Meanwhile, the WCB relies on those enterprises for innovation, labour for the citizens, efficient production, … that cannot be achieved through state administration alone. This structure ensures that no citizen goes without necessities, while still allowing room for private initiative, creativity, and economic diversity within Weltheas.

## Politics

## Councilship with a mix of Democracy

Our citizens know what is best for the nation, thus it is the Council sole task to uphold to the expectations of our Citizens. This means that the Citizens are able to have a direct influence on the Council. Citizens are able to meticulously craft laws, which they in turn can send to the Advisor, who acts as the mouthpiece between the Council and her Citizens. 

This means it is in the interest of the Council to stimulate her Citizens to craft said laws. This, however, does not exclude the possibility for the Council itself to craft laws, if need be.

## Non-classical Classicism

One of the central ideologies within Weltheas is the idea that Welthean citizens are the richest among all. These riches are not necessarily in the material sense, but simply being a Welthean citizen, broadens your position in the world and allows you to become what we consider a “Higher Being”. Of course, we strive for a mix of both material and psychological riches.

## Military and War

### Policies

The Free City of Weltheas and its Council realise that sovereignty and stability can not be ensured without security. While our foundations rest on economic coordination, we acknowledge that defence is an essential part of the state.

### No-Strike-First Doctrine

Weltheas should aim to adopt a No-Strike-First Doctrine as a guiding military principle. This means that:
- Weltheas shall NEVER initiate the first strike in an armed conflict
- Military forces shall only be used when absolutely necessary:
	- a direct attack on Weltheas
	- a verified imminent threat 
	- a formal defence obligation towards an ally being attacked 

This doctrine should exist to build international trust and signal that Weltheas is a stabilising power rather than an expansionist one.

However, it should be clear that Weltheas is not pacifist. Absolute pacifism would leave our citizens and allies vulnerable. We recognise that the refusal to act in the face of aggression can enable instability. Therefore:
- If Weltheas or any of its allies are being attacked, we will respond decisively.
- Defensive action may extend beyond our borders if required to neutralise ongoing threats.
- The objective of any military engagement shall be restoration of peace and security
